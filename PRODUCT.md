## Platform

web

## Users
Primary: recruiters and hiring managers at agencies, studios and product companies, mostly outside Benin, reading in English. They usually arrive from a job application, LinkedIn or Behance, have little time, and need to judge quickly whether Meryl can do the job: quality of craft, range, seniority and how she works.

Secondary (not the priority): prospective freelance clients. The current copy is largely written for them (see Open decisions).

## Product Purpose
A personal portfolio for Meryl Agnikpe, Web & UI/UX Designer. It exists to get Meryl hired: show selected work, prove experience across companies, and make contact effortless. Success means a recruiter understands her level and range within a minute and reaches out (email, phone or LinkedIn).

## Positioning
- **End-to-end designer.** Brand identity, UI/UX and the website itself, delivered by one person (logo and guidelines → interface → shipped site).
- **Fast delivery.** Most projects are completed in 1–3 weeks (stated in the FAQ).
- **3D with Spline.** Interactive 3D visuals as part of the toolset, which few web designers offer.

## Operating Context
- Visitors usually evaluate the site alongside a CV, a LinkedIn profile and Behance (behance.net/merylagn), often on desktop, sometimes on a phone from a LinkedIn link.
- Project case studies link out to Figma prototypes and a Google Drive guidelines PDF; recruiters may open them.
- Meryl maintains the original site in Framer and asks for changes to be carried over to this HTML rebuild.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Single-page static HTML/CSS/JS site with hash routes: home, project detail (`#/project/<slug>`), contact. No build step, no backend.
- Hosted on GitHub Pages: https://meryl924.github.io/Portfolio-Meryl-A/ (repo Meryl924/Portfolio-Meryl-A, branch main). `site-github/` is the deploy clone; edits are made in `portfolio-v2/` then copied over.
- The contact form opens the visitor's email app (mailto); nothing is stored or sent by the site.
- Tools: Figma, Framer, Spline 3D. Services offered: website design, UI/UX design, logo & branding, no-code development.
- Language: English only for now.

## Product Principles
1. **Work first.** A recruiter should reach real project images within one scroll; everything else supports the work.
2. **Prove the range.** Each case study should make the end-to-end claim visible: brand, interface and site, not just screens.
3. **Only true claims.** No invented clients, numbers, testimonials or results. Unconfirmed facts are flagged until Meryl confirms them.
4. **Contact in one step.** Email, phone and LinkedIn are always one click away.
5. **The site is a work sample.** Its own craft (layout, type, motion, performance) is evaluated as part of the portfolio.
